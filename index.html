What the model is

A small three-class softmax regression model trained in-browser from bundled educational examples.

What it is not

ProofPath is not a truth detector. The falsification layer exists to make that limitation visible.

Model status

Claim ceiling: EDUCATIONAL_EVIDENCE_ASSESSMENT_ONLY. Not a scientific validator, truth detector, medical/legal tool, or general automatic fact-checking service.
